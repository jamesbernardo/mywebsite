How I work

I don't stick to one channel. I build the thing, put it in front of people, then fix it based on what they actually do.

Marketing

I run Garage Society's marketing from the website out: the Webflow site, search, content, social, email, events and partners. I've kept one brand across our locations in Hong Kong, India and Southeast Asia.

Programmes and community

I design programmes that get startups, companies and government in the same room. That includes Garage Academy, our events programme, and projects like LaunchPad and CONNECT. I've also mentored startups and spoken at TEDx.

Building things

I build my own sites, small web apps and automations with AI tools, Git, Vercel and Airtable. At work I've run product roadmaps and CRM and ERP rollouts, and I'm usually the one translating between developers and the rest of the business.

Running a small team

When the market turned, I restructured our team into a much smaller one and kept the service running for members.

Tools I use

Webflow, Google Analytics 4 and Search Console for the site and search. Mailchimp for email. Airtable and OfficeRnD for CRM and operations. Git and Vercel for sites I code myself, and Claude and other AI tools for drafting, building and automating. I taught myself all of it by doing, and I'm comfortable working alongside developers without being one.
